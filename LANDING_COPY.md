## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to connect my accounts?

No. StackSpend does not connect directly to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, GitHub, or other providers.

The audit works entirely from the subscription and spend information you provide manually.

---

### How are recommendations generated?

All financial recommendations are deterministic and rule-based.

The system evaluates:

* plan pricing
* seat counts
* overlapping tooling
* usage patterns
* team size

to identify realistic optimization opportunities.

AI is used only for concise narrative summaries — not financial calculations.

---

### Why is there no login requirement?

The goal is reducing friction.

Users should be able to generate value immediately without account creation. Email capture only appears after the audit results are shown.

---

### What happens after I submit my email?

You receive:

* a copy of your audit report
* a public shareable report link
* future optimization updates if relevant

For higher-savings audits, Credex may optionally reach out regarding deeper optimization opportunities.

Primarily:

* startup CTOs

* AI-native product companies
* technical operators
* founders managing rapidly growing AI tooling costs

especially teams that adopted AI tools quickly without centralized spend management.
